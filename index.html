How to wander

WASD / arrows to walk, Shift to move faster. Drag the world to look around; scroll to zoom. Press Enter to chat, Escape to return to walking, M to mute.

Use Travel to visit the tea hills, old coast or backwater tea shop. Open Emotes to wave, sit or dance. Walk near the tea table and press E to play five questions together.

Share your room code with one friend. Voice fades as you walk apart and returns when you meet. Use headphones when testing two tabs.

If voice is blocked, text chat still works. Remote devices need an HTTPS page for microphone access. Restrictive networks may prevent a direct peer connection.
